About HORD

HORD is a personal website organizer provided by HORD Studio. This policy describes HORD's own features, not the privacy practices of the independent websites you visit. V1 is free, without in-app advertising or purchases.

Your collection stays on your device

HORD stores saved website names and URLs, categories, favorites, visit counts and recent-visit times, layout positions, and app preferences in local app storage. HORD does not provide an account, cloud synchronization, analytics, or a developer-operated collection server. Site icons and bundled fonts are local; opening the dashboard does not send saved domains to an icon provider. App storage is not an encrypted password vault: avoid saving credentials, access tokens, or confidential links. Android app backup is disabled.

Opening websites and fetching titles

Opening a website makes network requests to that website and the services it loads. Those services may receive your IP address, browser/device information, page requests, and information you enter, and may use cookies or other tracking under their own policies. HORD enables JavaScript and local website storage so pages can function. Third-party cookies are disabled on Android, but this does not block all tracking. Tapping Fetch page title also requests the selected page and may follow its redirects; sharing a link into HORD does not automatically fetch its title.

Website sessions and clearing data

Website cookies, cache, and local storage may persist in the embedded browser between visits. Settings > Clear data resets your HORD collection and app preferences; it does not explicitly erase website cookies, cache, or sessions. Removing a saved site does not sign you out of that website. To remove HORD's app storage and embedded browsing data on Android, use the system app-storage clearing controls or uninstall HORD. Separate browsers, websites, exported files, and services retain their own data.

Sharing, clipboard, and backups

If you share a link into HORD, the app processes the URL and any supplied title or text to prepare a saved entry. Copy URL writes the current URL to the system clipboard only when requested. Export data sends a JSON copy of your collection and preferences to the destination you choose in the system share sheet; that destination's policies apply. Exports are not encrypted by HORD and can contain private URLs. Import replaces the current HORD collection with the selected backup. HORD does not upload exports automatically or delete copies you have shared.

Support and your choices

If you email parth7378785501@gmail.com, the developer receives your message, email address, and information you choose to include. These messages are used to respond to support or privacy requests, not for advertising or sale. You can request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any necessary legal retention. Do not send passwords, identity documents, or sensitive browsing records. You can edit or remove saved entries, export your collection, clear app data, or uninstall. There is no HORD user account to delete.

External content and changes

Saved websites are independent services, and HORD does not endorse them or guarantee their content, accessibility, safety, or availability. Review each website's policies before signing in or entering personal information. This policy must be reviewed and updated before changes to HORD's data practices are released.
